The fill is densest just under the line and thins out step by step towards the baseline. A series that sits in front covers the ones behind it, so every area shows one texture. Series can overlap, stack on top of each other, or stack and scale to 100%. Move the pointer over the chart for a readout, and use the legend to spotlight a series.

## Data

The data is the same as for [DitherLineChart](../line-chart/): `categories` and `series`, each with `values`. The `low`, `high` and `dashed` fields are not used by an area chart; leave them empty. A transparent `color` takes the next colour of the series.
