Lines are drawn as smooth curves that never overshoot the data. A series can carry a range, such as p10 to p90 or a forecast interval, drawn as a dithered band around its line. Move the pointer over the chart for a readout, and use the legend to spotlight a series.

## Data

`categories` is the label of every point along the x axis. Every series has `values`, one number per category. Leave `low` and `high` empty, or give both the same length as `values` for a band. A transparent `color` takes the next colour of the series: ink, orange, green, pink, purple, blue, red, grey.
