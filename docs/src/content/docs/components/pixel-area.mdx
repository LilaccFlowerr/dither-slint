An area chart in the style of dither-kit: an ordered dither that is solid at the floor and dissolves upward, capped by a soft stepped edge at the value. Every pixel is the one colour at a different opacity, so it sits on light and dark backgrounds alike. Hovering lifts the whole fill a little and a marker reads the value under the pointer. A few stars sit in the fill and wink while the page is animated.
