---
title: DitherDissolveText
description: Text that dissolves in through the levels of a Bayer matrix, one character or word at a time.
---

import Demo from '../../../components/Demo.astro';

Each character, or word, is hidden by a dither that fills in over `duration` and settles solid, at a dither level, or into a dotted or hatched texture. Units dissolve in reading order, reversed, from the middle out, from both ends in, or shuffled. Set `showing: false` to dissolve the text out again.

As for [DitherText](../dither-text/), the dither is a layer over the text, so set `backdrop` to the flat colour behind it.
