---
title: DitherDonutChart
description: A donut or pie drawn in dither cells, with a solid rim on every slice and a sweep that draws it in clockwise.
---

import Demo from '../../../components/Demo.astro';
import Donut from '../../../components/Donut.astro';
import Api from '../../../components/Api.astro';

Slices are drawn clockwise from 12 o'clock in the order you give them. Each takes a colour and a texture and is capped with a solid rim. Hover a slice, or a row of the legend, to step it out and dim the others, and click to keep it lit. In a donut the middle shows the total, or the slice you point at. In a pie a small card sits over the slice.
