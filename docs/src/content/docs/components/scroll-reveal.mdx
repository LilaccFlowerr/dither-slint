The picture starts as a few large dithered blocks, sharpens step by step until it is dithered at one cell, and then dissolves cell by cell into the photo. Progress follows the scroll position, so scrolling back reverses it.

## Following the scroll

Slint does not tell a child where its scroll area is, so set `viewport-height` to the height of the area that scrolls. The picture then measures its own position against it. If the area does not start at the top of the window, set `viewport-top` too.

Or leave `viewport-height` at `0` and drive it yourself with `progress`, from 0 to 1.
