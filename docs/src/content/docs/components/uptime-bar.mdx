One bar per day, oldest first. The status shows in colour and in texture, so it never relies on colour alone: up days are a gradient that is dense at the base, degraded days hatched, outages solid and days without data dotted. Hover, tap or arrow through the bars for each day's details. The row shows the last 30, 60 or 90 days, depending on its width.

## Properties

<Api file="dither-uptime-bar" />

A day is up at 99.9% or more, degraded at 99% or more and an outage below that. Set `status` with `custom-status: true` to decide it yourself. The uptime in the header is the average of the days with data, and percentages are rounded down, so 99.999% never reads as 100%.
