dither-slint is a port of the dither work of [Mvkweb](https://github.com/Mvkweb), live at [dither-sdk.vercel.app](https://dither-sdk.vercel.app), made with their permission. The look, the components, the effects and the engine are theirs. The Rust and Slint code here is a port, not a copy.

Mvkweb's work is itself based on [dithercn](https://github.com/opencoredev/dithercn), and credits [Dither Kit](https://www.tripwire.sh/dither-kit) by Boring Software for the ideas behind its fills and charts, which in turn credits [EvilCharts](https://evilcharts.com).
