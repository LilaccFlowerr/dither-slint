A rotating dithered globe: land from a built-in mask, a lit terminator and a thin atmosphere, with pulsing markers and arcs between places. Drag it sideways to spin it and up or down to tilt it, or press the arrow keys while it has focus. A quick flick keeps turning and slows down, and the idle spin comes back afterwards. The globe stays round and centred in its box.

```slint
DitherGlobe {
    markers: [{ lat: 48.9, lon: 2.3 }, { lat: 40.7, lon: -74 }];
    arcs: [{ from-lat: 48.9, from-lon: 2.3, to-lat: 40.7, to-lon: -74 }];
}
```
