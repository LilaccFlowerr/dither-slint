A sphere whose outline breathes slowly, with a soft shadow side, a specular highlight and a dithered glow. The orb stays round and centred in any box. Move `lean` and the orb and its light tilt towards that side, easing there over a third of a second.

| Property | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `color` | `DitherTheme.ink` | Colour of the orb. |
| `highlight` | transparent | A third, brighter colour for the specular highlight. Transparent keeps the highlight in `color`. |
| `background` | `DitherTheme.background` | Colour behind the orb. Transparent floats it over what is behind. |
| `size` | `0.26` | Radius as a share of the box's shorter side, 0.1 to 0.5. |
| `morph` | `1` | How much the outline wobbles. 0 is a perfect sphere. |
| `lean` | `{ x: 0, y: 0 }` | Where the orb and its light tilt to, each axis -1 to 1 from the centre. Drive it from a `TouchArea` to follow the pointer. |
| `glow` | `0.5` | Strength of the dithered glow around the orb, 0 to 1. |
